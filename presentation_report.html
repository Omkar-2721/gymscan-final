Chapter 1: Problem & Aim

1. Project Overview & Problem Statement

The dietary supplement and sports nutrition market has experienced exponential growth globally, valued at over \$160 Billion in 2025. However, this massive demand has led to widespread industry malpractices, predatory marketing, and chemical adulteration that pose serious health and financial risks to gym-goers, athletes, and fitness enthusiasts.

The 4 Critical Adulteration Crises in Fitness Nutrition:

🚨 1. Amino Spiking (Nitrogen Fraud)

Manufacturers add cheap non-protein nitrogen compounds (Glycine, Taurine, Alanine, Creatine) to falsely inflate total nitrogen readings during standard Kjeldahl protein tests. A product claiming 25g protein often delivers less than 11g of true muscle-building intact whey.

⚠️ 2. Heavy Fillers & Diluents

Cheap carbohydrate bulking agents like Maltodextrin, corn syrup solids, and hydrogenated palm oil fractions are secretly added to powders and protein bars, causing rapid insulin spikes, bloating, and fat accumulation while diluting active ingredients.

⛔ 3. Banned & Dangerous Stimulants

Underground pre-workout and fat-burner formulas hide prohibited, dangerous compounds such as DMAA, DMHA, and Ephedra inside misleading "Proprietary Blends", leading to severe cardiovascular strain and athletic doping bans.

🧪 4. Synthetic Dyes & Irritating Sugar Alcohols

Excessive use of artificial food dyes (Red 40, Yellow 5) and high-glycemic sugar alcohols like Maltitol in "zero sugar" protein bars causes severe gastrointestinal distress, diarrhea, and allergen reactions.

Project Aim & Core Mission:

🎯 The GymScan Mission

GymScan was conceived to solve the information asymmetry between supplement brands and everyday consumers. By combining on-device Computer Vision (OCR) with a heuristic clinical nutrition engine, GymScan enables users to snap a photo of any supplement tub or snack label on their phone and receive an instantaneous, objective laboratory verification report within 2 seconds.

Chapter 2: System Architecture

2. System Architecture & Technical Pipeline

GymScan utilizes a 100% client-side, privacy-preserving, zero-latency processing pipeline. No sensitive user photos are transferred to external third-party cloud servers, guaranteeing complete privacy, zero hosting API costs, and instant offline-ready speeds.

End-to-End Execution Pipeline:

1
Image Acquisition & Adaptive Canvas Preprocessor
Captures label photo via HTML5 getUserMedia API or gallery picker. Normalizes 12MP/48MP smartphone photos on an in-memory 800px Canvas to prevent mobile CPU stutter and memory spikes.
2
WebAssembly (WASM) Optical Character Recognition (OCR)
Tesseract.js WebAssembly worker executes bounded OCR extraction (2.5s timeout bound) directly on device, transcribing typography, nutrition tables, and ingredient lists into clean raw text.
3
Universal NLP Label & Brand Classifier
Inspects text against 60+ global/Indian brands (Optimum Nutrition, MuscleBlaze, Bolt Nutrition, GNC, Dymatize, Quest, etc.) or extracts dynamic packaging headlines for unlisted brands.
4
Multi-Category Nutritional & Adulteration Heuristic Engine
Screens ingredient tokens against curated dictionaries (Amino Spikes, Banned Stims, Fillers, Synthetic Dyes, Clean Markers). Extracts macros and computes 0-100 Purity Score.
5
Reactive UI, Showdown Matrix & Bilingual Verdicts
Renders interactive clinical assays, macros breakdowns, side-by-side comparison winner banners, and English/Hindi clinical verdicts with 1-click WhatsApp sharing.

Mathematical Purity Scoring Formula:

📐 Mathematical Formulation

Purity Score (S) = Base (92) - ∑(Penalty_i) + ∑(Bonus_j)
Bounded between [15, 99].

  • Amino Spiking Penalty: -28 points per detected free amino acid token.
  • Banned Stimulant Penalty: -40 points per prohibited athletic stimulant.
  • Filler & Diluent Penalty: -18 points per maltodextrin/corn syrup entry.
  • Maltitol / Dye Penalty: -22 points for high-GI polyols in snack bars.
  • Clean Quality Bonus: +4 points for Creapure, Sunflower Lecithin, Stevia, Isolate.

Presentation Q&A / Viva Defense (Top 5 Questions & Answers):

Q1: How does GymScan detect Amino Spiking when brands claim high protein on the label?
Answer: Standard Kjeldahl lab tests measure total nitrogen, not intact amino acid peptide chains. GymScan's NLP engine scans the ingredients list for added free nitrogen compounds (Glycine, L-Taurine, Alanine). When found in combination with concentrate whey, the system flags the product and reduces the estimated True Intact Protein ratio to 40-55%.
Q2: Why does GymScan process OCR on the client-side instead of using Cloud APIs (like Google Cloud Vision)?
Answer: Client-side WASM OCR provides 3 key advantages: (1) Zero server API costs, allowing scalable free usage; (2) Total user privacy since photos never leave the phone; (3) Instant offline capability and near-zero network latency.
Q3: How does the system prevent mobile browser freezing on 48MP smartphone photos?
Answer: We implemented an in-memory Canvas downsampler that dynamically normalizes any high-resolution image to an optimal 800px dimension with 85% JPEG quality before invoking the WASM worker, paired with a strict 2.5-second execution bound.
Q4: What happens if a user scans a brand that is NOT in the database?
Answer: GymScan uses a Universal Dynamic OCR Line Extractor that filters out boilerplate text (like "Nutrition Facts", "Batch No") to dynamically isolate the packaging headline, brand name, exact nutritional numbers, and raw ingredients for ANY product globally.
Q5: What is the future commercial monetization model for GymScan?
Answer: A Freemium Model offering 5 Free Scans upon email registration, followed by micro-subscriptions (₹99/month for unlimited scans) integrated with Razorpay/Stripe, plus B2B verification partnerships with certified supplement labs.
